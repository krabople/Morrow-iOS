# Listello Support

Listello is designed to keep tasks simple and private. If you need help, have feedback or want to report a problem, please [open a support request](https://github.com/krabople/Morrow-iOS/issues/new) in the public issue tracker.

When reporting a problem, please include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, Listello version and the steps that caused the issue. Do not include private task or calendar information.

## Common questions

- **Calendar events are not visible:** Open iPhone Settings and allow Listello full Calendar access, then enable **Show Calendar Events** in Listello's Schedule tab.
- **A Reminders list will not import:** Open iPhone Settings and allow Listello full Reminders access, then return to **Settings → Import from Reminders**.
- **A notification did not appear:** Confirm notifications are allowed for Listello in iPhone Settings and that the task has a future scheduled time.
- **A completed task disappeared:** Recurring tasks move to their next occurrence. Other completed tasks may be moved to the Archive according to the option in Settings.
- **Restore archived tasks:** Open Settings, choose **View Archive**, then restore one task or all tasks.
- **Lists versus projects:** Lists use “items,” default to no duration estimate and stay out of All Tasks. Projects use “tasks” and the default duration. You can change either behaviour when editing the project or list.
